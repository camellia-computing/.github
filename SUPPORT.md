# Support

The Camellia repositories are currently pre-release engineering projects, not a promise of general availability or a commercial support SLA.

- Use repository issues for reproducible defects and narrowly scoped feature proposals.
- Use documentation and existing issues before opening a duplicate request.
- Do not use issues for security vulnerabilities, account recovery, credentials, private customer data, or urgent operational incidents. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for security reports.
- Maintainers may close requests that cannot be reproduced, are outside the product boundary, require support for obsolete pre-release state, or lack enough information to investigate safely.

Repository visibility, an artifact, or possession of source does not alter the applicable license or create a warranty or support obligation.
